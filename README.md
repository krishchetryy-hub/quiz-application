# quiz-application
Futuristic Quiz App is an interactive web-based quiz platform designed to test your knowledge across multiple subjects such as General Knowledge, Science, Mathematics, History, Geography, and more.Users can log in with their name and enrollment ID, choose their preferred subject, difficulty level, and number of questions (5, 10, or 15), and then take a timed quiz where each question has a 15-second countdown.The quiz awards coins for every correct answer and displays your final score at the end. You can also switch between dark and light themes for a personalized experience.Built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, this app provides a smooth, modern, and responsive user interface that works seamlessly on desktop and mobile browsers.
